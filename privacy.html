Last updated: October 30, 2026

This policy explains what information the PVS Johns Creek Pet Club (“the Pet Club”) collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The Pet Club is offered by [Clinic legal name], located at [Clinic address], Johns Creek, Georgia.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, and, if you give it to us, your mobile number.
  • Pet details: your pets’ names and species.
  • Program activity: invoice or visit numbers, the type of appointment, eligible spend amounts, points earned and redeemed, your level, and rewards used.
  • Recipes you submit: the title, ingredients, directions, and our review notes.
  • Technical information: sign-in and security records, such as your device, browser, and IP address, which our hosting and sign-in providers handle to keep the service working and secure.

The Pet Club is a rewards program. We do not keep veterinary medical records in it.

How we use it

  • To create your account, send you sign-in links, and show your points, level, rewards, and offers.
  • To add points after visits and purchases, apply rewards at checkout, and keep accurate records.
  • To review recipes you submit and award points.
  • To prevent fraud and misuse, keep the service secure, and meet legal and accounting requirements.
